Deal types

Four ways to work together

Open to any of these per brand, or a mix depending on what fits.

Option 1

Gifting

Product in exchange for agreed content. Lowest cost to test the fit — best for new relationships or product-led brands.

Option 2

Flat fee

Fixed rate per package, paid regardless of performance. Predictable for both sides, scoped by deliverable count.

Option 3

Rev share / affiliate

Commission on sales via a tracked link or discount code. Low upfront cost for the brand, upside tied to real performance.

Option 4

Hybrid

Smaller flat fee or gifting, plus rev share on top. The most common structure for ongoing partnerships.

On tracking: rev share and hybrid deals run on a unique tracked link or promo code per brand, so results are visible to both sides in real time — no guesswork on attribution.
